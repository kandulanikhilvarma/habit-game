A creature that grows from the habits you actually keep.

Kumo turns the habits you keep into a creature that grows with you. Which habits you keep decides what it becomes, and no two lives make the same creature. It gets sleepy, not angry, and when you come back it wakes up.

  • A creature, not a checklist. Miss a day and it waits for you. Never a guilt screen, never a streak you have to fake.
  • Your habits choose its shape. Mind, body or order: the blend you actually live picks which of four branches it evolves down.
  • Yours to keep. Works offline, plays without an account, and you can export or delete everything at any time.

Play Kumo. Free, no account needed.
